## 🚀 Key Features & Capabilities

The platform is designed with a "Google Sheets meets Notion" philosophy, ensuring that operations require minimal clicks and operate at lightning speed.

### 1. Centralized Command Dashboard
* View all active, planned, and completed Yatras in a single interface.
* Real-time statistics displaying confirmed devotees, total amounts collected via UPI, and outstanding payment balances across all Yatras.

### 2. Tabbed Yatra Workspace
Every spiritual tour operates as a self-contained workspace, eliminating page reloads and keeping all relevant information organized under easy-to-navigate tabs.

### 3. Hotel & Accommodation Research Tracker
* A spreadsheet-style module to record hotel evaluations (distance to temple, room prices, extra mattress costs).
* Toggle statuses for `Contacted`, `Shortlisted`, and an exclusive `Final Selected` toggle that automatically highlights the chosen accommodation.

### 4. Seamless Devotee Registration (with 15-Day Expiry)
* **Public Links:** Instantly generate shareable registration URLs for each Yatra.
* **Deadline Enforcement:** Registration links automatically expire after 15 days (customizable), seamlessly blocking late entries.
* Collect critical participant data including family size, strict dietary requirements (e.g., Sattvic, No Onion/Garlic), and essential medical notes.

### 5. Automated UPI QR Payments & Verification
* Upon registering, devotees are presented with a dynamically generated UPI QR Code tied directly to the organizer's bank account.
* Devotees upload their transaction screenshot and reference number securely.
* Organizers can verify or reject payments with a single click from the dashboard, instantly updating the devotee's confirmation status.

### 6. Intelligent Expense Splitter Calculator
* Track all Yatra expenses (Transportation, Food, Temple Donations).
* Allocate expenses to `Everyone`, `Selected Families`, or `Selected Individuals`.
* The system’s algorithm automatically calculates per-person shares (factoring in family size), subtracts their verified payments, and instantly calculates exactly who has an outstanding deficit.

### 7. Integrated WhatsApp Communication
* One-click "Welcome", "Payment Reminder", and "Verified Receipt" buttons directly inside the Devotee table.
* Automatically opens WhatsApp Web or the WhatsApp App with a pre-formatted, personalized message containing the devotee’s name and yatra details.

### 8. Self-Service Devotee Portal
* Devotees log in using simply their registered phone number.
* They can view travel schedules, upload proof of payments, and instantly update their own profile details (which syncs immediately to the Admin's dashboard).
* **Photo Sharing Gallery:** Devotees and organizers can upload photos. The organizer has a one-click "Download All Photos to ZIP" feature to easily archive memories.

### 9. Export & Printable Reports
* Export all financial data and participant lists directly to CSV format for external auditing.
* Dedicated formatting for clean printing.
